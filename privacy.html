Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

HomeGameGo ("we") is a neighborhood sports app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider)To create your account and sign you in
Display name and optional profile photoSo teammates and players can recognize you
Your age confirmation and whether you're a parent, coach or playerTo apply age-appropriate rules
Games, scores, check-ins, court queue entries and your home courtTo run games and leaderboards
Team and club memberships, team-chat messages, event RSVPs and comments, direct messagesSo you can organize with your teams and contacts
Photos and videos you choose to add (profile, Locker gear, courts, teams, clips)To show them in the app
Connections you make in person by scanning a QR codeTo link families and players who know each other
If you apply to join a youth club: the street address you type, and its map coordinatesSo the club can see whether you live in its neighborhood. This is your address as an adult, never a child's.
Fundraising entries: pledge and contribution amounts and names you enterTo keep a club's or team's fundraising ledger. HomeGameGo never collects or moves money.
Reports you submit about other users' contentTo review and act on them

Location. The app uses your device's location only on your phone, to center the map when you add a court. Your location is not uploaded or stored. Camera. Used only to scan QR codes and take photos you choose to add.

Children

Children under 13 never have their own account and can't sign in, post or message. A parent or legal guardian creates a sub-profile for their child inside their own account. For a child we store only:

  • A nickname the parent chooses (no real name; a parent may optionally share the child's first name with the child's coaches only, and can turn that off at any time)
  • Birth year and month (no full date of birth), and the resulting age band
  • Sport interests, the clubs the child is on, session attendance, and games the child played
  • The consent documents the parent signed for the child's club

We never store a child's address, phone, email, school, precise location or medical information. A child's profile is visible only to their guardians and the coaches and rosters of the clubs they're on. Children are never searchable. A parent can review, edit or delete their child's profile at any time in the app, and can contact us to do so. We collect a child's information only with the parent's consent, given in the app.

Teens aged 13 and over may have their own account. HomeGameGo doesn't allow private messaging between adults and minors.

Who can see your information

  • Other HomeGameGo users: your display name, photo, games and leaderboard results; your messages are visible to the team or person you sent them to.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase to host our database, sign-in and file storage, on servers in Canada (Amazon Web Services, ca-central-1). They process data only to provide that service.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and don't share it for advertising.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app at any time (Profile → Settings → Delete Account); here's exactly what that removes. Records other people rely on, such as games you played, are kept but show "Deleted player", with no link to you.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by database rules so each person sees only what they're allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit most of your information in the app, and delete your account there. To request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of a child's information, email info@homegamego.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
