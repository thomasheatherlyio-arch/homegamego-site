Delete your HomeGameGo account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account, type DELETE, and confirm. It takes effect immediately and can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email info@homegamego.com from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in, name, email and profile photo
  • Your direct messages, connections, blocks and Locker items
  • Your clips and share links
  • Your team and club memberships, RSVPs, and any club application (including the address you entered)

Your children's profiles

  • If you share your household with a co-guardian, your children's profiles stay with them.
  • If you're the only guardian, your children's profiles are deleted too. A child who appears in other people's game or check-in records is kept only as an anonymous, inactive "Former member", with no nickname, rosters or sports.

What stays, anonymously

Records other people rely on, such as games you played, leaderboards, team-chat messages and club records, stay so their history isn't rewritten. They show "Deleted player" instead of you, with no link to your name, email or photo.
