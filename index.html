Support

HomeGameGo is a neighborhood sports app: check into a court, play and log games, keep up with your teams, and run a local youth club with the parents and coaches you know.

Contact us
Email info@homegamego.com. We reply within two business days, and review safety reports within 24 hours.

Report a problem with another user

In the app, press and hold a message, then tap Report. You can also Block someone from the same menu, or from the menu at the top of a conversation. Blocking stops messages both ways and hides their team-chat messages from you. Manage blocked people in Profile → Settings → Blocked People. For anything urgent, email us as well.

Joining

HomeGameGo is invite-only. Ask the person who told you about it, or your club's coach, for an invite code.

Kids and families

Children under 13 never have their own account. A parent creates a sub-profile for their child, identified only by a nickname. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what's stored.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time. See how account deletion works.
